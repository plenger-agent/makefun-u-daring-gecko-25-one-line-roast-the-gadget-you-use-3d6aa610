The entry:

> My earbuds case dies faster than the earbuds — it's a tiny coffin with a charging port.

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes that single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## Why this line

The judge weighs, in order: a daily gadget clearly roasted, a joke that lands on first read, a fresh angle, neat wording, and one person's specific gripe with a concrete detail. The earbuds case hits all five: everybody charges earbuds daily, the case dying before the buds is a sting of recognition, "a tiny coffin with a charging port" is a comparison the judge is unlikely to have seen, and at 87 characters it is comfortably inside the 200-character gate — short also helps the smaller-text tie-break.
